Know which underpass floods.
Before the bus leaves.

Sentinel watches the rain forecast over 26 Delhi low points, drafts the alert with an AI agent, and tells dispatchers which route to send.

EXPLORE Photo: Connaught Place in rain, Vyacheslav Argenberg, CC BY 4.0

Every monsoon the same underpasses fill within an hour of heavy rain. Dispatchers get a city-wide forecast. They need an answer for one road, right now.

01

Forecast every low point

Hourly rain for each location is compared with its own threshold, so risk is per underpass, not per city.

02

An agent drafts, a human decides

An AI agent built on the open-source Strands Agents SDK from AWS writes the alert. Nothing is sent until a dispatcher approves it.

03

Send the safer route

Pick a trip and see which driving routes pass a flagged spot, and which one avoids them.

04

Learn from what happened

Each alert is checked against the rain that was recorded, and thresholds adjust after false alarms and misses.
